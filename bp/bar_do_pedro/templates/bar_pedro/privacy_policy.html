Privacy Policy

This policy describes what DrinkMeUp stores when you use the website or the mobile API. We do not sell your data.

Data we collect

  • Account: username, email, and a hashed password if you sign up.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose Google, we receive the profile and email Google shares for that login so we can create or recognise your account.
  • Preferences: taste, spirits, and boozy level you submit to get a suggestion.
  • Drink history: cocktails you mark as made, plus optional rating and comment.
  • Guest use: preferences for a visit are kept in a session cookie on your device, not in an account.

Cookies and sessions

We use a session cookie so you stay logged in, so guests keep their last order, and so security features such as CSRF protection work. The mobile app uses access and refresh tokens instead of a browser session.

Email

If you ask to reset a password, we send a reset link to the email on your account. We do not use your email for marketing.

How long we keep data

Account and drink history stay until you delete the account. You can delete your profile from the site; that removes your user record and drink history.

Your choices

  • Use Guest mode without creating an account.
  • Change your password from your account.
  • Delete your profile from the authenticated menu.
